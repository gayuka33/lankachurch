# **App Name**: Praise Prayer Connect

## Core Features:

- Church Info Hub: Display church information: about, services, gallery, news, contact (address, phone, email, social media), and donation.
- Service Schedules: Show church service schedules: Sunday service, Sunday school, Hymns practice, WhatsApp prayer, and Prayer request submission form.
- Prayer Request Submission: Enable users to send prayer requests.
- Donation Information: Provide links to donation platforms or instructions.
- AI Jesus Chatbot: AI Chatbot: a tool that answers questions and interacts with users in Sinhala, Tamil, and English. Simulate conversations with Jesus.
- AI Bible Verse Search: AI-powered Bible verse search: allows users to search for specific verses by title, chapter, and verse in Sinhala, Tamil, and English languages.
- Responsive Design: A landing page or series of landing pages that are modern, responsive and follow basic UX/UI design principles.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vivid orange (#FF9500) to represent energy and warmth.
- Background color: Light green (#E8F5E9), a very desaturated tone for a calm background.
- Accent color: Fiery red (#FF3B30) for calls to action and important notifications.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for a balance of modernity and readability.
- Use simple, clear icons for navigation and services, ensuring they are accessible and understandable.
- Ensure the layout is responsive across all devices. Content should be well-organized and easy to navigate.
- Subtle transitions and animations on navigation and interactive elements to enhance user experience.
